## Notes

- **InstPitch**: 16-bit instrument pitch multiplier ($0100–$3FFF). Doubling = +1 octave.
- **RootKey**: MIDI note at which the sample plays at native 32 kHz rate.
- **FineTune**: Cents correction for rootKey rounding (applied via SF2 fineTune generator).
- **ADSR1/ADSR2**: Raw SNES DSP envelope register values.
- **Atk**: Attack rate field (0–15). 15=instant, 0=slowest.
- **Dec**: Decay rate field (0–7). 7=fastest, 0=slowest.
- **Sus Lv**: Sustain level as fraction of peak.
- **Sus Rate**: Sustain fade rate (0=infinite hold, 31=fastest fade).
- **Loop**: Whether the sample has a loop point (sustaining instruments).
