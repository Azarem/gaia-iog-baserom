## 1. Method

- **Stage** (`stage` field of the record): `act<N>.<area>` from the area of the record (`db-us/groups.json` group of the scenes that own the flag) with overrides for the engine, Dark Space and jeweler flags (`any.*`) and the dungeon kill table (`transient.dungeon_kill`). Acts: act0 Prologue (title, prophecy narration); act1 South Cape, Edward's Castle, Itory, Inca Ruins; act2 Gold Ship, Freejia, Diamond Mine, Nazca, Sky Garden; act3 Seabed Palace, Mu, Angel Village, Watermia, Great Wall; act4 Euro, Mt. Kress, Native Village, Angkor Wat, Dao, Pyramid; act5 Babel Tower, Comet, ending.
- **Milestone** (M01-M29): the story order comes from the world-map exit gates of `overworld_exit.asm` and the scene-warp / `QueueMapChange` edge graph (`fl32_scenes.mjs edges`, 709 edges, every scene except `$FF` reachable from the boot scene). A flag belongs to the milestone of its area unless `fl32_decisions.json` overrides it with a flag-level reason (the first South Cape evening, the castle letter, hotel and second-visit flags, Gorgon hut flags, bosses, statues, abilities). The registry schema has no milestone field: the milestone of each flag is the table below and `_milestones` in the fragment; the `stage` carries the act and area.
- **Prerequisite edges** (`requires` / `excludes`): a must-analysis of every setter site. For each literal `SetFlag*` site the guard set is the set of flag tests that hold on every path from the actor entry to the site (`BranchOnFlag*` taken or fall-through, `WaitOnFlag*` completed). A flag requires `X` when every setter site is guarded by X set, and excludes `X` when every site is guarded by X clear. These are **set-time guards** (the condition under which the setter runs), not permanent invariants: a flag that is cleared later can lose the relation. Flags written by helpers or tables (469 indices) or with several meanings get no derived edge. Sites in `unused/` are ignored.
- **Scene entry**: the joypad-lock writes (`TSB/ORA/STA $joypadMaskStd`) of every actor file a scene places (engine actors under `actors/` excluded), reached from the actor-def entry without a halt (an arrival lock), with the guards that hold; classified against FL20-FL29 scene-entry tables and the cited actor files. `cutscene` = an arrival script locks the joypad and releases it by itself; `flags` = the minimum persistent flags that skip it.
- **Presets**: the flags of all milestones up to the target (D2 story and D3 event flags, ability flags from the ability list, boss flags of earlier bosses), minus optional flags and flags that story code clears; key items from the FL40 give/remove events simulated into inventory slots (first empty slot); form Will; gems 0.
