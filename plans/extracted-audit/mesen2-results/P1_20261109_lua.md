## 3. Facing probe (OQ-28)

`p1.facingProbe` (kit) writes `$09AE` bit 15, `$0AC8` and `$1028` at the entry exec mark and restores them at the three `RTL` marks. O layout below; R = each offset minus 2907. Button: O `b` held 150 frames (interact bit is physical B in the identity-map state `orig_S01r`), R `a` pulsed 45 times (2 frames down, 4 up). `raw/P1_face_O.txt`, `P1_face_R.txt`:

```lua
local L={entry=258250,fallback=258286,override=258289,tbl=258305,dataLoad=258319,ret={r1=258281,r2=258285,r3=258334},fot=258423,lookup=258335,data={258431,258458,258502,258506},len={27,44,4,6},endBlock=258512}
local plan={}
local function add(n,f,b) plan[#plan+1]={n=n,f=f,bit15=b} end
for f=0,3 do add(0,f,false) end
add(0,1,true); add(1,2,true); add(2,3,true); add(3,1,true)
for _,f in ipairs({0,1,2,3,12,23,24,25,26,27}) do add(4,f,true) end
for _,f in ipairs({0,1,2,3,43,44}) do add(5,f,true) end
for _,f in ipairs({0,1,3,4}) do add(6,f,true) end

local S=p1.facingProbe{L=L,plan=plan}
m1.run(function(m)
  m.wait(4)
  gaia.hold({'b'}); m.wait(150); gaia.release(); m.wait(10)
  m.log('calls=%d forced=%d plan=%d marks: fallback=%s override=%s tblLoad=%s dataLoad=%s r1=%s r2=%s r3=%s', S.calls, S.forced, #plan, tostring(S.marks.fallback), tostring(S.marks.override), tostring(S.marks.tblLoad), tostring(S.marks.dataLoad), tostring(S.marks.r1), tostring(S.marks.r2), tostring(S.marks.r3))

  for i,r in ipairs(S.rows) do
    m.log('%02d n=%d f=%d b15=%s path=%s xTbl=%s ptr=%s data=%s byte=%s A=%d C=%d ret=%s caller=%s', i, r.n, r.f, tostring(r.bit15), r.path, tostring(r.xTbl), r.ptr and string.format('%04X', r.ptr) or '-', r.dataOff and p1.classify(L, r.dataOff) or '-', r.byte and string.format('%02X', r.byte) or '-', r.A, r.C, r.ret, r.caller)

```

Row-8 check (`raw/P1_face_O_wram.txt`): plan n = 8 (f 0..3), 9 (f 0,1), 15 (f 0,1) and an extra exec mark at 258319 predicting the effective address (the long-indexed load adds X to `$F177` and carries into the bank):

```lua
emu.addMemoryCallback(function()
  local st=emu.getState(); local x=st['cpu.x'] & 0xFFFF
  local sum=0xF177+x
  local low=sum & 0xFFFF
  local carry=sum>0xFFFF
  local v
  if carry then v=emu.read(low, emu.memType.snesWorkRam) else v=emu.read(0x3F177+x, emu.memType.snesPrgRom) end
  pred[#pred+1]=string.format('x=%04X eff=%s%04X pred=%02X', x, carry and '84:' or '83:', low, v)
end, emu.callbackType.exec, 258319, 258319, emu.cpuType.snes, emu.memType.snesPrgRom)
```

Result: `x=0F89 eff=84:0100 pred=F4` = the returned A (244). An earlier run of this check used the wrong base word (`$F1F7`) and predicted wrong bytes; the table above uses `$F177` (258423 = `$03:F177`).

## 4. Escort tracker (OQ-I052-7)

`orig_P02_before_incan_ruins.mss`, O, `raw/P1_escort_O.txt`, `P1_escort_O2.txt`:

```lua
local P=emu.memType.snesPrgRom; local CPU=emu.cpuType.snes
local C={entry=0, loop=0, seedRet=0, moveRet=0}
local res={}
local function rec(tag, st)
  local a=st['cpu.a'] & 0xFF; local c=st['cpu.ps'] & 1
  local key=string.format('%s A=%d C=%d f28=%04X', tag, a, c, kit.r16(0x1028))
  res[key]=(res[key] or 0)+1
end
local function mark(off, fn) emu.addMemoryCallback(function() fn(emu.getState()) end, emu.callbackType.exec, off, off, CPU, P) end
mark(51206, function() C.entry=C.entry+1 end)
mark(51342, function() C.loop=C.loop+1 end)
mark(51220, function(st) C.seedRet=C.seedRet+1; rec('seed', st) end)
mark(51505, function(st) C.moveRet=C.moveRet+1; rec('move', st) end)
m1.run(function(m)
  m.wait(4)
  local seq={'up','right','down','left','up','right','down','left','a','b'}
  for r=1,6 do
    for _,d in ipairs(seq) do gaia.hold({d}); m.wait(18); gaia.release(); m.wait(4) end
    m.log('round %d n36=%04X entry=%d loop=%d seed=%d move=%d pos=%d,%d mask=%04X pf=%04X', r, kit.r16(0x36), C.entry, C.loop, C.seedRet, C.moveRet, kit.r16(0x1014), kit.r16(0x1016), kit.r16(0x065A), kit.r16(0x09AE))
  end
  for k,v in pairs(res) do m.log('%s x%d', k, v) end
  return {entry=C.entry, loop=C.loop, seed=C.seedRet, move=C.moveRet}
end,{out='P1_escort_O.txt', maxFrames=1400})
```

(The second run, `P1_escort_O2.txt`, continued from the first run's end state with a 24-frame hold, the extra buttons `y` and `x`, and 6 rounds of 12 presses; it hit `maxFrames` after 5 rounds, before the A / carry listing was written. Its counters (seed 1, moving frames 9 in scene `$1D`) show 10 more tracker calls whose A and carry were not logged, so only the first run's 10 calls count as observed returns.)
