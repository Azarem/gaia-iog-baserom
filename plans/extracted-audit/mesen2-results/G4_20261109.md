## Script
`kit/lib/g4_kit.lua` (317 lines, SHA-1 `0ffe07278854230ccd7d6e5ca38879b8b839198c`) and `kit/lib/g4_runs.lua` (50 lines, SHA-1 `1e056150cd036bdffbaeab32731bda7a0cc64d83`), with `gaia_kit`, `a_census`, `a_actors`, `g1_kit`, `g2_kit`; drivers `harness/g4_sweep.py` (91 lines, SHA-1 `3f8ce1fa6f5d914296d90d4bd307a89fdfd55a4b`; one label of its `SITES` table was corrected after the run) and `harness/g4_tier.py` (38 lines, SHA-1 `d0e69317da07d0ff6a56fbb2766f2826d823c416`). They are the verbatim Lua of every callback (same practice as G1 / G2).
- `g4.static{file}`: ROM-only decode of the 110 rows and of every scene's spawn records (`frames = 1`).
- `g4.sweep{scenes, cap, file}`: per-slot write callbacks on `statsPtr` (`$7F0020,X`, WRAM `0x10020 + slot`; a 16-bit store is two byte callbacks, the high byte combines the pair and classifies the value: a table row when `(v - $ABF0) % 4 == 0` and the row < 110, else a histogram entry), per-slot write callbacks on the tier (WRAM `0x1101C + slot`), an exec counter on tag 249854 (the resolve), `g2.warper` (write `sceneNext` `$0642`, A pulses for the banner, 30 NMI frames). Every scene starts from one savestate saved and reloaded inside the NMI-stub exec hook at ROM 32779 (T-62). The scene's per-row counts of the spawn resolve (site 249858) are compared with the static per-scene enemy-spawn counts (`sceneMismatch`). A scene that does not finish in 800 frame callbacks is recorded as a fail; if the `INC $36` control does not change for 1,200 frame callbacks the run ends with `crashedAt` (the CPU no longer reaches the NMI stub: the game derailed after the warp).
- `g4.tier{frames, pulses}`: write callbacks on the tier byte of the 84 slots (site, value, count, first t, slot), exec counter on tag 55463 (the tier read of the stagger controller), `pulses` = button list held for N NMI frames.
- Invocations: `python harness/g4_sweep.py 1 255` (chunks of 32 scenes; `load_state_file` S10 before each chunk; the chunk after a `crashedAt` starts at the next scene) and `python harness/g4_tier.py orig_S07_diary.mss 300 diary "{{t=40,btn={'a'},len=4}}"` (`includes ["gaia_kit","a_census","a_actors","g1_kit","g2_kit","g4_kit","g4_runs"]`, `turbo true`).
