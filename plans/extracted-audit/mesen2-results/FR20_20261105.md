## Static prelude
- Flag convention and scene load: `ClearSceneState`'s `STX $eventFlags` (offset 253291) zeroes `$0A00-$0A01` only; the quick warp is a write to `sceneNext` (`$0642`) after the flags. Scene arrival = exec callback at offset 253291, then `$36` advancing.
- Collision cell byte: low nibble 0 free, `$F` solid terrain type, high nibble `$F` = actor solid mark (`MarkSolidHere`/`MarkSolidAbs`); read with `gaia.coll.get(tx, ty)`.
- `chatPtr` (interact handler stored by `SetInteractHandler`) is the word at `$7F000A + slot` (work RAM offset `0x1000A + slot`).

## Script
`kit/lib/fr20_kit.lua` (new this step): `fr20.visit{...}` is a state machine (`stage`, `loading`, `await36`, `banner`, `settle0`, `settle`, `done`). Parameters used: `scene`, `set`, `clear`, `post={set,clear}`, `settle`, `sampleEvery`, `hold`, `mash`, `walk`, `teleport`, `watch` (`gaia.flagwatch`), `maskwatch` (write callback on `$065A-$065B`), `readwatch` (read callback), `execs` (exec callbacks at ROM offsets), `cells`, `vram` (64 checksums of 1 KB VRAM chunks), `cgram`, `peek`, `hooks` (raw Lua). Result: flag arrays (`flagsPre`, `flags0`, `flagsBanner`, `flagsPost`, `flags1`), actor lists (`actors0`/`actors1`: slot, entry bank/pc, x, y, `$10`, `$12`, wait, `chatPtr`), `mask0/1`, `dmf0/1`, `timeline`, `scenes`, `maskRows`, `fw`, `execs`, `readRows`, `ctl36`.
The verbatim Lua of every run (kit call + parameters) is stored in the `lua` field of its raw JSON, `raw/FR20_<trial>_20261105.json`, together with the ROM identity and the named actor lists. The call form is always:

```lua
fr20.visit{ scene=6, set={22,27,61,62,28,29,33}, clear={37,38,281}, settle=120, watch=true, maskwatch=true,
            cells={{5,29},{6,29}}, vram=true, tag="S6_CASTLE" }
```
