## Static prelude
- Flag convention: global flag n is WRAM byte `$0A00 + (n >> 3)`, bit `n & 7`; the watch covers `$0A00-$0AB3`. Flag stores that can change a bit: SetEventFlag `STA $eventFlags, Y` at `system/engine/flag_helpers.asm:114` (offset 45265, callback end 45268), ClearEventFlag line 138 (45300, end 45303), `ClearSceneState` `STX $eventFlags` (253291, end 253294) and the `$FF` clear (253423, end 253426); the callback reports the PC after the instruction, so the row's `end` minus the instruction length is the store.
- Actor identification: inside a flag handler D = the actor slot and X = the bit index. Slot `+0..+2` is the re-entry address saved by the last yielding COP; the bank byte is the HiROM mirror (`$84` = bank 4), so ROM offset = ((bank & `$3F`) << 16) | pc, equal to the `; {n}` tag of the extracted file. The setter is the next SetFlagByte / ClearFlagByte with that bit operand after the entry.
- Flags `$0000-$000F` are cleared at scene load: scene-local flags 0-15 repeat per scene, so every row carries its scene.
- `$F4` (M01 `new_game_start`): the only writer on the title path is the COP at `system/title/sFC_title_intro.asm:49` (offset 772466) after the idle wait of 2516 ticks (`WaitWord #$09D3`); the Start button path leaves the title through the diary menu scene `$FA` and the church scene `$08`.
- Doors: a town door opens when the player point is within 16 px and Up is held; the door's actor mark (high nibble `$F0`) can cover the doorstep cell, so the walk uses the side cell (`enterDoor(... side)`). `sc06_hamlet` pins Will with `BranchIfPlayerAt` at actor position (`$0038`, `$01A0`) (player point (48,400)); `sc01_guard` and `ec0A_left_guard` check the player point in the gate area; `overworld_exit.asm`: leaving South Cape north (player Y < `$10`) loads the world-map menu `$FE`, whose cursor starts on Quit.
- Collision layer: cell 16 px, low nibble 0 free, F solid, 3 door/trigger, 1 passable stairs, 5/6/9 slopes, 8 ledge, high nibble F actor solid mark. The player point `$09A2/$09A4` is the top-left of a 16 x 16 hitbox; actor position = player point + (8, 16).
