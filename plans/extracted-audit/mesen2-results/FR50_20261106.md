## Script
Kit `kit/lib/fr50_kit.lua` (`-- requires: fr40_kit`, verbatim in `FR50_20261106_lua.md`): `fr50.arrive{scene, set, watch, input, dest, stopFree, tag, cg, walk}` wraps `fr40.arrive` and adds the snapshot of the preset as written (`s0`), at the banner end (`sA`) and at the end (`s1`) (form, gems, ability word, 16 item bytes, equipped, HP, jewels, `$0AA6`, `$0AAC`, the 180 flag bytes), the form history, every store that changes `joypadMaskStd` (frame, old > new, ROM offset of the writing instruction, actor slot and entry), the actor position every 10 frames, 16 palette checksums (`cg`) and a scripted walk (`walk = {{dir, from, to}, ...}` in watch frames). It writes no WRAM byte. Harness `harness/fr50.py`: `h1`, `preset`, `walk`, `form`, `inv`, `state`; the preset bytes are written with `write_memory` (work RAM: flags OR into `$0A00-$0AB3`, ability byte OR into `$0AA2`, items at `$0AB4`, form word `$0AD4`, gems word `$0AD6`) and read back (`ok` in every row) before each script.
Per preset three runs from the same loaded state, same frames, same input (A pulses, 2 frames of every 12), same warp: C (no preset bytes), P (preset), K (preset plus the scene's skip flags from `FL32_scene-flags.json`, where the scene has some). Warp: full warp with the natural entry of the scene where FR40 has one (`warpDestX/Y`, `warpFacing`, `warpScrollParams`, then `sceneNext`), quick warp for `$58`, `$DD`, `$E7`, `$E8`. Watch: 1500 frames after the banner, stopped 40 free frames after the first free run (`stopFree = 40`); `$58` 600 frames, `$E7` 1100 frames.
Call forms (full text in `FR50_20261106_lua.md`):

Buttons: physical A pulsed (the game maps it to the interact bit `$0080`); the walk presses D-pad directions in the same input poll. Select held for the inventory screen (`fr20.visit`). Frames are emulated frames; `d36` (writes of `$36` in the watch window) agrees with the watch length.
