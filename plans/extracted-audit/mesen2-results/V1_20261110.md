## Static prelude (file:line, `extracted/`; decimal ROM offsets)

- `system/engine/cop_handlers_wait.asm:3` ("a count of N resumes N+1 ticks later (0 = next tick)") and `:27` (`STA $08`, {44233}, shared tail of WaitByte/WaitWord).

## Predictions (written before each run; ticks follow plan rule 8: `WaitByte #n` = n + 1 ticks)

- A (door): the `WaitWhileOffscreen #0A` of a door re-runs 11 runner passes later, and 11 NMI frames, every time.
- B (palette): between two seeds of one palette actor the runner passes number the seeded wait byte + 1, in every strip, and equal the NMI frames.
- C (title): `WaitByte #B3` (= 179 decimal) resumes after 180 actor passes and `WaitByte #77` (= 119) after 120. In NMI frames the first may be longer if something blocks the main loop; the `ScreenEnterTransition` fade-in (135 frames, no pass) is the candidate.
- D (CL-04): setting `$2000` in the `$10` word of a visible non-player actor removes its entry from the sort (zero stores at {247420}) while the other actors keep one store per frame.
- E (CL-10): with tile 0 made non-black, `M7SEL` `$80` shows the backdrop (black) beyond the plane and `$C0` shows the tile 0 colour (white).

for _, a in ipairs({248615, 248674, 248769, 248828, 248921, 248983, 249116, 249138, 249238, 249260}) do
  onExec(a, function() local s = slot(); passes[s] = (passes[s] or 0) + 1 end)

onExec(50695, function()
  local s = slot()
  local prev = R.last[s]
  if prev then
    local row = R.iv[s] or {}

kit.onFrame(function()
  n = n + 1
  if n >= 390 then
