## Coverage and limits

- The recipe warp was run for scene `$0A` only. The quick-warp rows ran on O only (the quick warp on R is not yet measured, only the recipe).
- The matrix confirms scene id, mask and banner, and the screenshots were read by eye; it does not prove that each scene is playable.
- `step_scanline` has one timed row (`timeoutMs 3000`) and could not be shown to hang again; the hard watchdog (120 s) was never reached.
- The SPC test counts callbacks and reads three PCs; it is not a trace of the SPC command handlers (D2 does that).
- R-plain is the 16:32 build; a rebuild changes its SHA-1 and invalidates `R-plain_S01r_south-cape-ready.mss`.
