## Positive control

- `$36` write control (NMI `INC $36`): fired once per frame in every script (counts in `P1_20261109_lua.md`).
- OQ-22 control (facing order, plan): `FacingDirectionLookup[f]` for f = 0..3 returned A = 0, 1, 2, 3 with the carry clear, on O and R (rows 01-04 of the facing probe: down, up, left, right).
- Window trials: the exec marks of the four Walk entries and `SetAutoWalkTimer` fired in every trial; `E` (walk entries) was 2 or 3 in every trial.
- Facing probe: the entry mark fired 150 times (O, one press-hold of 150 frames) / 45 times (R, 45 pulses), 36 of them forced.
- Button sweep: every direction produced its own walk entry word; `L` / `R` produced the block stance entry (`$A1B0` on R, `$CA73` on O); Start and Select produced their overlays (scene `$FF` for the inventory).
