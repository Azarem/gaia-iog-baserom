## Partial answer to OQ-I026-4 (palette bundles `$10` and `$0E`)

Decoded with the throwaway bundle decoder (not committed). Bundle `$0E` is a single-frame load of 15 CGRAM words (destination field 241); bundle `$10` is an 8-frame CGRAM fade of 16 words (destination field 129) with 3 extra ticks per frame, and its last frame starts with the same 15 words that `$0E` writes (`$7D8C`, `$7E10`, `$7E94`, `$7F18`, ...): they are pale blue / lavender tints (for example `$7E10` = R16 G16 B31, `$7F18` = R24 G24 B31). So the old wording "desaturated" for the death-screen fade is wrong: the fade runs towards a blue-white tint, and `$0E` is the instant version of the end state. What is still unverified is which palette rows the destination fields address (sprite or background) and what the start colours of `$10` are on screen.

OQ-I026-4 is therefore partly answered; the remaining part is runtime-only.
