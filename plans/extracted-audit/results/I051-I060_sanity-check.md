# Sanity check of iterations I051–I060 — 2026-10-28

Scope: the 30 files of I051–I060 (see `checklist.md`), their notes under `notes/`, the ledgers and the gate. Method: as for [I041-I050_sanity-check.md](I041-I050_sanity-check.md): re-read every audited file in `extract:lt` form against the rubric of `plans/iog_extracted_asm_audit` (Q1–Q7) and the Comment Authoring Guide in `gaia-core/docs/notes-and-comments.md`; check each factual claim in the kept comments against the code (and, for engine behaviour, against the COP handler docs); run the integrity checks. Companion files: [I051_results.md](I051_results.md) … [I060_results.md](I060_results.md), [../renames/sanity-I051-I060_renames.md](../renames/sanity-I051-I060_renames.md), [../questions/sanity-I051-I060_questions.md](../questions/sanity-I051-I060_questions.md).

## Verdict

The code, the ledgers and the structure of the notes are sound, and the comment density is right (21–57 % of instruction lines, 40–60 % only in the tiny palette thinkers, `sFB_boot_logo` and the two title files, where nearly every line carries a magic number). The **facts** had one recurring defect: durations of `WaitByte` / `WaitWord` were stated as the operand `n` instead of `n + 1` ticks. This is the same defect the I021–I040 sanity pass fixed ("`WaitByte(n)` = n+1 ticks, stated as n"); the I051–I060 audits reintroduced it in 12 files. A second, smaller group: three stale words in comments (a "game over flag", an "altitude", a "player falls each frame") and one missing note after the user's `types.json` change. All are fixed: **28 comments, 23 part notes (22 rewritten, 1 new), 7 block notes** (counts from the merge report), 13 files.

## Observations (no change made here)

- **Tooling gap: orphan note.** `notes/partNotes/bank03.json:237` still holds `"loc_03B928": "Not code: the three bytes C2 11 CA …"`. The label no longer exists, so `merge` cannot remove it (`label not found`); it is ignored by extraction and is harmless. It can be deleted by hand (one line) when convenient; the new `dialogstring_03B928` note replaces it.
- **Tooling gap: `world_map_routes.asm` and the three `*.sprite.asm` files cannot take notes** (`merge`: "cannot determine the ROM bank of this file"; I055 R-I055-5). `world_map_routes.asm` therefore still carries the old header that reads the 4-byte steps as `flags, direction, ?, distance` — the reading `world_map_options` and `WorldMapController` have proved wrong (three movement-delta indices and a tick count). The correct description is in the `world_map_options` and `WorldMapController` block notes. The merge tool needs a bank source for files without a `?BANK` that start with data (or a `?BANK` line in those extracted files) to fix this.
- **Ledger wording:** `renames/I060_renames.md` R-I060-3 says the bytes after `code_03B922` are "the text Nothing!!". They are the **empty** `[TPL:11]` template (like `dialogstring_03B92B`); "Nothing!!" is `dialogstring_03B949`, printed by choice `$26`. The ledger is a frozen document and was not edited; the rename itself (`dialogstring_03B928`) has been done by the user's `types.json` change and is correct.
- **History narration:** 18 of the 30 block notes carry a "the old header said …" paragraph (the "CORRECTIONS" sections). They are accurate and useful for the audit trail but are not documentation of the current code; the authoring guide does not forbid them. Candidate for a later strip once the renames are done.
- **`sE6_gaia` `$0008` "grounded" comments** noted by the I041–I050 sanity pass were already corrected in I056 (comment {587509} says "collision-checked movement").
