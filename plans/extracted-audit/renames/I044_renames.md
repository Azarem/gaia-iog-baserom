Corrections for R-I044-5 (to apply in a later iteration that touches those files):

1. `docs/code/bank00/thinkers-palette.md` lines 211-231 (`incan_ruins_transform_palette`): the thinker is spawned in Gold Ship scenes `$2B` / `$2D` / `$2F` (the table's 'index 34 / 35 / 36 ... Incan Ruins exterior / interior, Angkor complex' is wrong); flags `$4D` / `$52` are set by `dc2F_adrift` (not 'mid-game transform' and 'final crystal phase'); the phase is chosen only at spawn; `#34` plays once and `#33` loops under the `$FF` latch.
2. `docs/code/bank00/README.md` lines 135 and 485 ('Angkor/Incan') and `docs/code/bank0C-scene-spawn-tables-comet.md` line 439 ('Larai Cliff area'): same correction.
3. `docs/code/bank00/functions-camera-motion.md` lines 203-228 (`ApplyOrbitalOffsetXY`): the fields are `+0` X angle, `+1` Y angle, `+2` X diameter, `+3` Y diameter (the doc has the Y angle at `$7F0012` and the diameters at `$7F1010` / `$7F1012`); 'about 3 boss / actor scripts' = 3 call sites (2 in `gw8A_sand_fanger`, 1 in `awB1_goldcap`); the routine adds to the current `$14` / `$16`.
4. `docs/code/bank00/thinkers-system.md` lines 282-350 (`global_ambient_dispatcher`): the trigger is the interact bit (not 'the player presses a direction button'); the actor scan is a probe point 1 px in front with |dx|, |dy| below `$10` (not 'within 16x16 pixels'); 'DMA sound #002F to APUIO' is the actor snapshot / restore around the chat routine (`$2F` is the MVN byte count); palette bundles are loaded once at spawn for the player's OBJ palette 0 (not 'ambient lighting'); clearing bit 11 of `animScratch2` (`$7F000E,X`) removes the thinker from the Light runners; the labels need the corrections above.
5. `docs/code/bank00/data-tables-memory.md` line 566, `docs/code/bank00/README.md` lines 866 and 910, `docs/code/bank0C-scene-spawn-tables-comet.md` line 430: the scene counts (`~40`, `9`, `59x`) disagree; the spawn tables show 59 lists covering 173 of the 184 scenes that have thinkers.
6. `docs/wram-memory-map.md` section 2.9 (Input Remapping): the 'Init Value' column lists the vanilla defaults; this build's `system_init` writes `remapB = $4008`, `remapA = $8080`, `remapY = $0040`, `remapX = $0004` (db-us/transforms.json), which is what makes the A button feed the interact bit `$8000`.
