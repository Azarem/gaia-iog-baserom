Corrections for R-I047-6 (to apply in a later iteration that touches those files):

1. `docs/code/bank00/actors-combat-interaction.md` lines 120-170 (`e_hp_increase`, `e_str_increase`, `e_def_increase`, `RewardActorVFX`): `$0ACA` is MAX HP (not "HP") and `$0ACE` is the CURRENT HP (not "base HP"); the actor adds 1 to max HP and sets `hpToHeal` to new max minus current; the `$0255` clamp is never reached (`BVC` skips it unless the sum overflows signed 16 bits); the actors are spawned by `EnemyStatBonusReward` with `SpawnListAppend` and flags `$1000` after it sets `displayModeFlags` `$0080` (display filter), not "SpawnLastRel after combat victories"; `RewardActorVFX` is called with `CallNear` (not `CallScript`) and the pickup flies to the player with `StageMove` / `TickMove` (it does not "snap" to the player position).
2. `docs/wram-memory-map.md` line 293: `displayModeFlags` bit `$0080` is "music sequence"; the engine treats it as the display filter (only actors with Flags10 `$1000` run), which the reward actors rely on.
3. `docs/code/bank00/actors-player-rewards.md` lines 272-300 (`inventory_statue_slot`): `$0AFA` = 3 (Status tab) HIDES the statue; every other tab shows it ("highlighted / visible" is reversed); the table has 4-byte entries.
4. `docs/code/bank02/player-movement.md` line 140: `player_move_controller` is in bank `$02`, not bank `$00`.
5. `extracted/actors/player/slope_ramp_physics.asm` header (line 3): "turns those speeds into movement ... on the next pass" is imprecise: the list order is attack, player, slope, controller, so the controller reads the speeds in the SAME frame, right after the slope actor.
6. `docs/wram-memory-map.md` Flags10 / actor-flag tables (if present): `$0008` is "collision-checked movement" (not "grounded"), `$0080` is "iframe counter running" (not "freeze"), `$0040` is "dead". Check the table in the next doc-touching iteration; not read in this iteration.
